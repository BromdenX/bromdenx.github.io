Build carefully. Learn in public. Keep the useful parts.

I am BromdenX, an engineer interested in practical AI systems, network infrastructure, and the tools that connect them.

My work here is less about following individual products and more about understanding how systems behave in practice: how problems are framed, how information moves, how tools cooperate, and how one useful experiment becomes a repeatable workflow.

What I write about

  • AI & Agents: multi-agent work, shared memory, workflows, and Personal AI OS experiments.
  • Networks & Systems: infrastructure, self-hosted services, automation, and troubleshooting.
  • Life & Learning: methods, changing assumptions, and observations worth keeping.

This site is a working record rather than a catalogue of finished answers. I try to preserve the problem, the reasoning, the implementation, and what changed afterward.

Public projects and code are available on GitHub.
